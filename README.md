## 📖 SOP Documents

### 1. SOP BAHAN BAKU (Raw Materials)
- Raw material receiving procedures
- Storage standards (FIFO/FEFO system)
- Quality control & stock monitoring

### 2. SOP DEEPFRYER
- Deep fryer operation & maintenance
- Temperature standards (160-180°C)
- Frying times for different chicken types
- Oil management & replacement schedule

### 3. SOP KEBERSIHAN (Hygiene)
- Employee personal hygiene standards
- Workplace cleanliness procedures
- Equipment cleaning protocols
- Waste management

### 4. SOP PELAYANAN PELANGGAN (Customer Service)
- Dine-in & Take Away procedures
- Online delivery (GoFood/GrabFood/ShopeeFood)
- WhatsApp order handling
- Complaint resolution

### 5. SOP POINT OF SALES (POS)
- Order input procedures
- Payment processing
- Receipt printing

### 6. SOP PENYAJIAN (Food Serving)
- Food preparation workflow
- 15-minute service standard
- Portion control

### 7. TAKARAN SAJIAN (Menu Specifications)
- Detailed portion sizes (grams, pieces)
- Packaging requirements for 40+ menu items
- Recipe standards

### 8. SURAT JALAN (Delivery Document)
- Delivery note template
